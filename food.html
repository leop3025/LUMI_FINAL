Practice What You've Learned

1. How would you say "I would like the chicken" in Spanish?

Quisiera el pollo
Quiero el pescado
Me gusta la carne
Dame el pollo
Correct! "Quisiera el pollo" is the polite way to order chicken.
Not quite. The correct phrase is "Quisiera el pollo" which means "I would like the chicken".

2. What would you say to ask for the check?

¿Dónde está el baño?
La cuenta, por favor
¿Qué hora es?
Más agua, por favor
Excellent! "La cuenta, por favor" is how you ask for the check.
Actually, "La cuenta, por favor" is the correct way to ask for the check.

3. How would you tell the waiter you're vegetarian?

Tengo hambre
Soy vegetariano/a
No me gusta la carne
Quiero verduras
That's right! "Soy vegetariano/a" clearly states you're vegetarian.
Not exactly. The correct phrase is "Soy vegetariano/a" when stating dietary preferences.
