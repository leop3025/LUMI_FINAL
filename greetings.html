Greetings & Introductions

Learn basic Spanish greetings and how to introduce yourself

Basic Greetings

Hola

Hello

Used at any time of day

Example: ¡Hola! ¿Cómo estás? (Hello! How are you?)

Buenos días

Good morning

Used until noon

Example: Buenos días, señor. (Good morning, sir.)

Buenas tardes

Good afternoon

Used from noon until sunset

Example: Buenas tardes, ¿cómo está? (Good afternoon, how are you?)

Buenas noches

Good evening/night

Used after sunset or when going to bed

Example: Buenas noches, hasta mañana. (Good night, see you tomorrow.)

Introducing Yourself

¿Cómo te llamas?

What's your name?

Response: Me llamo [name] / Soy [name]

Mucho gusto

Nice to meet you

Response: Igualmente (Likewise)

¿De dónde eres?

Where are you from?

Response: Soy de [country/city]

¿Cómo estás?

How are you?

Responses:

  • Muy bien, gracias (Very well, thank you)
  • Bien, ¿y tú? (Good, and you?)
  • Regular (Okay)

Practice What You've Learned

1. How would you say "Good morning" in Spanish?

Buenos días
Buenas noches
Hola
Buenas tardes
Correct! "Buenos días" is used in the morning.
Not quite. "Buenos días" is the correct phrase for "Good morning."

2. What is the appropriate response to "¿Cómo te llamas?"

Bien, gracias
Me llamo Ana
Soy de España
Mucho gusto
Excellent! "Me llamo [name]" means "My name is [name]."
Actually, "¿Cómo te llamas?" is asking for your name, so you should respond with your name.

3. Which greeting would you use when meeting someone for the first time?

Adiós
Mucho gusto
Buenas noches
Hasta luego
That's right! "Mucho gusto" means "Nice to meet you."
Not exactly. "Mucho gusto" is the appropriate phrase when meeting someone for the first time.
